Recent history

GHB, GBL and 1,4-BDO can cause sudden unconsciousness and dangerous breathing suppression. Combining them with alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines, sleep medicines or other depressants can sharply increase risk. If someone is hard to wake, breathing slowly or irregularly, vomiting while unconscious, turning blue/gray, or having a seizure, call emergency services and do not leave them alone.
